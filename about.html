Boat rental service in Trogir and Split

We are a Croatian company based in Trogir, Croatia, since 2009. During the last 15+ years we've been trying to make vacations for our customers as good as possible.

We always hear that the customer comes first. Our motto is that "the customer is always right." We listen to every compliment and every piece of feedback, and we keep improving every year.

In addition to chartering, we are also very successful in boat transfers. Our boat rental and transfer service is available in Trogir, Split, Brač, Hvar, Šolta, Sevid, Primošten, Omiš, Rogoznica and many other places in Dalmatia.
